## Splito - Making Crypto feel like Money

Splito makes group payments seamless by letting users split expenses in fiat and settle in crypto across chains, tokens, and wallets.

- Split in fiat with no volatility or manual conversions
- Settle in crypto with each user receiving funds in their preferred token and chain
- Use wallets or emails to join splits
- Splito handles who gets what, where, and how

Preferences are resolved in order: split -> group -> profile. <br />
Fiat values are locked at the time of creation; settlement is triggered by a settler, not forced.
